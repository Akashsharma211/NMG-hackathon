## 5. PixVerse Features Used & Why
*   **MultiShot:** Utilized to maintain character consistency across different angles during the intense dialogue scenes in the library.
*   **Image Region Editor:** Used to precisely adjust the lighting and shadow details on the protagonist's face without altering the complex background of the college environment.
*   **Extend:** Crucial for lengthening the final establishing shots to match the pacing of the indie-pop background score.

## 6. Iterations (The Honest Process)
*   **What Failed:** Initially, the prompts for the campus scenes were too generic, resulting in flat, stock-footage-looking videos. The characters lacked emotional depth in early renders.
*   **What Changed:** Shifted the prompt engineering to focus heavily on lighting cues (e.g., "cool blue cinematic lighting") and camera angles (e.g., "low angle close-up"). Relied more heavily on the Image Region Editor to manually fix expressions rather than re-rolling entirely. Honed in on a specific, stylized color grading to unify the scenes.
